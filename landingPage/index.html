How CampusPay Works

Four simple steps from account setup to item collection.

01

Sign up & Verify with NIN

Create your student account in under 60 seconds and complete identity verification with your National Identification Number (NIN).

02

Fund Your Campus Account

Transfer money from any bank app to your dedicated personal CampusPay account number to top up your balance.

03

Select Item & Enter Matric Number

Pick your department handout or fee, enter your matric number, and enter your 4-digit PIN to pay instantly.

04

Scan QR Code on Distribution Day

Present your digital QR pass on collection day. Your Course Rep scans it once with their phone to confirm your pickup.

Every student profile is verified via National Identification Number (NIN) lookup upon registration to ensure complete transparency, prevent impersonation, and keep all departmental payment records 100% accurate.
