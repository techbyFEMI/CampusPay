How It Works

Four Simple Steps from Signup to Collection

The complete campus payment flow — from first account creation to QR-verified item distribution day.

01

Sign In with Matric Number & Verify NIN

Enter your matriculation number to get your full name automatically pre-loaded from the class roster. Then verify your identity with your National Identification Number (NIN) and set your secure 4-digit PIN.

02

Fund Your Campus Wallet Account

Transfer money from any bank app — GTBank, First Bank, OPay, Kuda — to your personal CampusPay wallet account number. Funds arrive instantly and are ready for 1-click payments.

03

Select Item in Departmental Catalog

Browse course handouts, lab manuals, or department dues listed by your Course Rep. Pick your item, confirm with your matric number and 4-digit PIN, and the payment clears instantly.

04

Scan QR for Distribution Confirmation

On collection day, show your digital QR pass on your phone. Your Course Rep scans it once with their camera to confirm your distribution — no paper receipts, no lists, no confusion.

NIN Identity Verification & Secure Transactions

Every student profile is verified via National Identification Number (NIN) lookup upon registration — ensuring complete transparency, preventing impersonation, and keeping all departmental payment records 100% accurate and audit-ready.
