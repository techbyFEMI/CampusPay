How it works

01

You list what's needed

Post the item and price — a handout, a textbook, a class contribution. Whatever you're already collecting for.

02

Classmates pay with their matric number

No signup forms, no writing their name on a sheet. Their name pulls up automatically from the class list.

03

You buy, once everyone's in

Funds sit held until you're ready to make the purchase — you go buy with what's actually been collected.

04

Scan to confirm on distribution day

One scan checks each person off. No paper list, no "did you pay?" arguments at handover.

Why it still works the way you'd expect

Matric numbers are checked against your actual class list, so a name only shows up if it's really one of your classmates. You're still the one who decides what's listed and when — this just replaces the sheet of paper, not your job.
